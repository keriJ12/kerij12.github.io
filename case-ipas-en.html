iPAS Practice + AI Study Companion — ask what the question tests before giving the answer

A learning loop for candidates of Taiwan's iPAS “AI Application Planner” (entry level): a pure front-end practice site serves original questions by the seven official exam topics, each with a three-layer explanation — key concept, recognition signal, distractor shape — and files wrong answers into a notebook. Export the notebook and hand it to the “study companion”, who works through it one question at a time, Socratic style, to find out whether the candidate missed the concept, the rule, or was pulled away by an option. All questions are original; none are official exam items. The UI is in Traditional Chinese.

01Context

The setting

Preparing for a new certificate, where practice material is scarce or of unclear origin

iPAS AI Application Planner is a new Taiwanese competency certificate; the entry level has two subjects, AI fundamentals and generative-AI application planning. Candidates need to practise against the official exam scope, but official items are copyrighted and third-party banks blur the line between real and rewritten questions. I was a candidate myself and wanted a practice tool that also got “how to review a wrong answer” right.

02Problem

Problem statement

A wrong answer has three causes; a single explanation serves all three the same text

You can miss what the question tests, know the concept but not the rule, or know the rule and still be pulled away by an option. The remedies differ. Typical “explanations” only address the first case. So the goal was: structure every explanation so it can support a diagnosis, and have the companion ask first, teach second, and finally make the candidate say it back.

Scope

Built — and deliberately not built

Built

  • Questions by the seven official topics and content codes
  • Three-layer explanation: concept, signal, distractor shape
  • Wrong-answer notebook (removed after one correct answer), mastery per topic
  • Notebook export as JSON for the companion
  • Companion: one question at a time, three-level diagnosis tree, variant question, closing advice
  • Pure front end; accounts and progress stay on the device

Deliberately not built

  • Official past exam items or any hint of “this was on the exam”
  • Backend, payments, cross-device sync
  • Pretending a live AI exists on a static site
  • Psychological profiling of the learner

03Practice site

Decision · Categories

Use the official exam topics, not invented chapters

The seven topics map directly to the official exam scope and each item carries a content code, so mastery tells the candidate exactly which chapter to reread. The star rating (definition / scenario-sequence-regulation / reverse-trade-off-metric) is our own item-type scale, labelled on screen as not an official difficulty.

Decision · Explanations

Every item has the same three layers: concept, signal, distractor shape

“Why it is correct”, “which phrase in the stem is the clue”, and “how the wrong options mislead” are written separately, so the experience of one item transfers to its variants. The same three fields are the only evidence the companion is allowed to cite.

Decision · Originality

All items original — and visibly so

Publishing past items would be a legal risk and would imply “this is what will be asked”. Items are written against the content codes, the site is labelled “original practice items, not official” in several places, and even the source label makes each item recognisable as a simulation.

Decision · Accounts

Password hashing even for a local-only account, plus a warning not to reuse real passwords

Accounts and progress live in the browser. Passwords are still hashed with PBKDF2 (120,000 iterations, per-account salt) in case someone reuses a real password, and the screen says plainly not to. The portfolio version adds a “try it” entry: data lives only in the tab and disappears when it closes.

04Companion

Decision · Opening line

“Don't look at the answer yet. Do you know which concept this question is testing?”

One question at a time, one prompt at a time, then stop. No concept → supply the concept in plain words. Concept but no rule → ask for the core rule. Rule known → ask “then why not the option you chose?”, pointing at the stem with the signal and dismantling the option with the distractor shape. The diagnosis stops at the level reached.

Decision · Confirming learning

Say it back in your own words, then a variant with the same concept in a new scenario

Only a correct variant moves on; two misses mark the item for another review. The close lists the codes to reread, three oral recall prompts without answers, and what to practise tomorrow.

Decision · Red lines

The companion may cite only the item fields and the course excerpts

The biggest risk of a generative model is getting regulation numbers and years wrong. Restricting its knowledge to item data and course excerpts turns it from encyclopaedia into tutor and shrinks the error surface. When unsure it says “the notes don't cover this, check the official announcement”; it never answers for the candidate, never dumps the whole explanation, and keeps every reply under twelve lines.

Decision · Honesty

On a static site it plays a script, labelled in three places

The companion has two modes: live conversation where a model is available; elsewhere a fourteen-message scripted demo, marked “scripted demo, not live AI” in the badge, a notice bar and an in-chat system message. Rather than wire up an API that breaks or fake replies, the conversation design itself is the exhibit.

05Tech & boundaries

Technical notes

Zero external CDN, bundled fonts, four small modules

  • Structure: storage (the only module touching storage) → auth → selection and scoring (pure logic, no DOM) → view and events.
  • Bundled fonts render fully even from file:// and leak no user IPs to a font provider.
  • Dark mode is manual and applied before first paint, so dark users never see a white flash.
  • Try-it entry: `?demo=1` switches storage to sessionStorage and creates an account that cannot be logged into by password; logout wipes it. The hashing code for normal accounts is untouched.
  • Notebook format: the JSON carries its own instructions for an AI, so any chat model can start from it.

06Limits

Limits

Eight demo items, no backend, and the companion here is a recording

The portfolio version ships eight original demo items, so mastery and the notebook show the mechanism, not statistics. Accounts and activation codes live in the browser and anyone can edit their own state in dev tools — this is a flow demo, not a membership system. The companion page uses two hard-coded wrong answers and “export” only starts the script; no model is connected on the static site. Not affiliated with iPAS; all exam information comes from the official site.
